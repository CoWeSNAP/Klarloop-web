Privacy Policy

for the Klarloop app and the website klarloop.com · Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung (the German version is legally binding)

In short

  • Klarloop works without an account. Your progress, your creature’s name and your settings stay on your device.
  • Ads (Google AdMob) are only shown the way you allow in the consent dialog. Personalised ads require your consent.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are only sent if you explicitly agree. You can change this anytime in the app under “Your mind”.

2. Data on your device

Progress, high scores, settings and your creature’s name are stored only locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. Daily reminders are local notifications.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

The app is free and funded by ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). On first launch, Google’s consent platform (UMP, IAB TCF) asks for your consent; on iOS, Apple additionally asks whether the app may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). Depending on your choice, Google processes IP address, device and app information, the advertising ID (only with permission) and ad interaction data. Personalised ads and storing/accessing information on your device require your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can change or withdraw your choice in the app under “Your mind” → “Privacy settings for ads”, and the IDFA permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Anonymous usage statistics (only with consent)

If you agree, we send anonymous information such as chapter, world reached, score, accuracy, app version and language, together with a random identifier that is not linked to your name, device or advertising ID and is deleted when you withdraw consent. Purpose: improving the game. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw anytime under “Your mind” → “Anonymous statistics”. Provider: TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany; processing in the EU.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store; we receive no payment data. To recognise whether you bought “Ad-free” (also after reinstalling, via “Restore purchases”) we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), which processes a random app identifier, Apple’s purchase receipt (product, time, country/currency), device and app information and your IP address – no name, email or payment data. Purpose: providing your purchase. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on appropriate safeguards (EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

6. Website

klarloop.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website uses no cookies, analytics or ads.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Locally stored game data can be deleted in the app under “Your mind” → “Reset progress”.
